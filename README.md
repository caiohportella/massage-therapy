## 📋 About

**Ritha Portella** is a modern and welcoming landing page developed for an integrative massage therapist. The project aims to present the services offered, highlight the benefits of massage therapy, and facilitate appointment scheduling through direct WhatsApp contact. The design was carefully crafted to convey calmness, well-being, and energetic healing, utilizing soft color palettes, glassmorphism, and fluid animations.

## 🔋 Features

### 🌸 **Professional Presentation**
- **Integrative Profile:** Detailed introduction about the professional and her journey in Traditional Chinese Medicine.
- **Visual Highlight:** Design focused on images and elements that convey tranquility and trust.

### 💆‍♀️ **Services Catalog**
- **Interactive Carousel:** Fluid navigation between different types of massages and therapies offered.
- **Therapy Details:** Clear descriptions of how each treatment can help (pain relief, anxiety, etc).

### ✨ **User Experience (UX/UI)**
- **Smooth Animations:** Utilization of Framer Motion for elegant and dynamic screen entrances.
- **Responsive Design:** Perfect experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
- **Glassmorphism:** Modern UI elements that provide a premium and sophisticated look.

### 📅 **Streamlined Booking**
- **WhatsApp Integration:** Direct action buttons for contacting and scheduling appointments.
- **Direct Booking Section:** Strategic call-to-actions positioned throughout the page to maximize conversions.

### 🗣️ **Social Proof & Partnerships**
- **Testimonials:** Dedicated area for client feedback to build credibility.
- **Partners:** Section to display commercial and institutional partnerships.

